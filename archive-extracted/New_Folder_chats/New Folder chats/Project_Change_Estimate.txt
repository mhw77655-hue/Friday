32. And how far can that ultimately go?
Here's my honest ceiling.
Near-term / realistic:
A very capable personal AI organism with persistent identity, memory, multimodal perception, voice, social intelligence, device control, research, coding, learning, and cloud/phone workload migration.
Absolutely attainable as an ambitious engineering project.
Advanced:
A system that has genuinely accumulated years of personalized experience and has learned your environment, people, habits, workflows, language, devices and preferred ways of doing things.
Very plausible.
More advanced:
A system that independently discovers some of its own limitations, researches solutions, builds candidate improvements, tests them, and safely upgrades itself.
Technically plausible in bounded domains. This is where I would expect meaningful progress long before anything resembling unrestricted recursive AGI.
Extreme:
An autonomous artificial entity whose learned internal representations, world model, social model and developmental process become substantially different from the design its creators originally wrote.
That's the frontier. Possible in principle, but not something I would promise as a guaranteed outcome.
Consciousness:
Unknown.
I wouldn't fake certainty there.
