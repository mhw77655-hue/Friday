Each experiment gets two materially different mechanisms, the smallest test that tells them apart, a way to replace them, and a trigger to dissolve them. All of it is HYPOTHESIS.

**1. Ears** (VOICE-1)
- **A:** an offline recognizer as a second ear, picked by a rule.
- **B:** keep only Android's recognizer and repair its mistakes using her capability list and your history. For example, "open you tube" becomes the YouTube launch.
- **Test:** about 30 clips of your own voice with typed transcripts, in English, Egyptian and mixed, split into commands and sentences. Score each way on word error, command success, latency and battery.
- **Replace / dissolve:** both sit behind the same speech port. Dissolve A if it doesn't beat B on command success for its extra cost. Dissolve B if the repair rate is negligible.

**2. Conversation** (VOICE-2)
- **A:** a state machine with fixed timeouts and barge-in.
- **B:** the same skeleton, but she learns when you're done speaking from your repairs. Examples are you talking again within a second of her reply, or "I wasn't finished".
- **Test:** a week of daily use. Count how often she cuts you off or talks over you under A versus B.
- **Replace / dissolve:** a learned policy replaces the state machine only if cut-offs actually fall. Otherwise dissolve B.

**3. Hands** (HANDS-1)
- **A:** app-specific routes (app launch, alarm intents, media session) with a check on the result.
- **B:** screen-level control. She reads the screen and taps or types through the existing Shizuku bridge, so no app-specific API is needed.
- **Test:** the same five outcomes (YouTube, alarm, music, notifications, a drafted message) by both routes. Compare verified success, latency, brittleness when an app updates, and permission cost.
- **Replace / dissolve:** per outcome, whichever route wins keeps it, and the other stays a fallback. Dissolve a route that fails the same outcome twice.

**4. Change from experience** (next, after the Seed Report)
- **A:** learned weights at existing decision points (response shape, retrieval order), updated online from your evidence.
- **B:** a small model trained offline in the workshop from her logged episodes, then adopted with lineage.
- **Test:** DEV-1 run with both, across a restart, a time gap, different wording, and the other language, with both controls (replay blocked, mechanism off). Then a second unrelated domain.
- **Replace / dissolve:** dissolve A or B if it fails the two-domain test. Dissolve B if the workshop round trip never beats A.

**5. Same goal, different means** (next)
