Try: episode A changes her and becomes unreachable. B arrives in different words, in the other language, after a restart and a time gap, and she handles B differently. The two controls are replay blocked and mechanism off. The evidence is your approval, corrections, demonstrated preferences, what you do next, and what actually happened, including contradicting evidence.
Claims but doesn't: experience reaches behavior only as text put into the prompt, or through six Human Core trait numbers. Those numbers anneal to near-stasis after about 29 updates and only pick canned openers. No prediction is ever formed on the live path.
Proof / breaks: the difference survives blocked replay and vanishes with the mechanism off, in two unrelated domains with no domain-specific code. It breaks if it passes only when the old sentence reaches the prompt (that's memory) or has to be told the domain.
Replaces → emerges: the first guesses are shifts in which alternative she picks, learned weights on retrieval and routing, and small adaptive models. These could lead to habits, expectations and tastes nobody wrote.
Depends / verdict: real decision points where behavior can vary, of which few exist today. Mutate until two domains pass, and dissolve the Human Core tables if they lose.

3. THINGS STANDING OUT AND COMING TO MATTER (attention, salience, expectation, curiosity, her own relevance)

Needs:
What pulls her attention is shaped by what mattered before.
Expectations get violated and the surprise is kept.
Things she noticed herself can reach you, and your reaction teaches her.
Try:
Salience drift: after contexts where X led to outcomes you cared about, X pulls her sooner in a new one.
Cheap predictions of when you'll ask and what you'll want, settled by what happens.
At most one unprompted mention a day, from unfinished things or patterns, withdrawn when it keeps missing.
Claims but doesn't:
Attention and salience scoring exist as hand-written rules.
Curiosity and proactiveness are only numbers in a trait table.
Companion behaviors have no caller.
The prediction piece serves the builder only.
Proof / breaks: she raises something unprompted that you later confirm mattered, traceable to her history. It breaks if initiatives are timers or random, or never improve with feedback.
Replaces → emerges: hand-written weights replaced by learned ones, leading to curiosity as a pull toward what reduced her uncertainty before, and to concern.
Depends / verdict: areas 2 and 10. Mutate, and dissolve the trait numbers.

4. FEELING AND INCLINATION (affect, values, preferences, attachment, stable individuality)

Needs: persistent, fading internal state whose meaning is learned from consequence and relationship. It colors attention, tone and willingness across many processes, and no part owns "emotion". Valence and arousal may be the wrong axes.
Try:
A residue test: a hard exchange leaves a measurable trace hours later (more careful, shorter) and fades with repair.
Preferences formed over weeks that she can trace to episodes and revise.
Different handling of people, grounded in shared history.
Claims but doesn't:
Emotion is English word counting.
Mood is two numbers with a fixed half-life.
The guard is 77 phrases.
Trust is scalars from phrase tables.
Proof / breaks: tendencies that are traceable to history, stable and revisable. It breaks if affect is announced in words but changes nothing later.
Replaces → emerges: whatever reproduces residue with fewer assumptions wins, leading to values and tastes.
Depends / verdict: areas 2, 3 and 5. Dissolve the tables when residue beats them.

5. BEING WITH PEOPLE (relationship, mutual adaptation, understanding others, your sister)

Needs: every episode links to who was involved, with uncertainty about who. A relationship is what that history has made, it changes, and what belongs to one stays there.
Try:
The same ambiguous sentence from you and from your sister is read differently, for reasons she can state.
Relationship drift after repair or conflict.
She learns about your sister from what you tell her and uses it later, with no hand-written sister record.
Claims but doesn't:
Per-person records and a four-level trust enum are profiles, not histories.
The Human Core holds a second trust.
Nothing recognizes who is speaking beyond what's declared.
Proof / breaks: different readings of identical words, traceable to different histories, with no leakage. It breaks if the difference is a swapped profile.
Replaces → emerges: profiles become relationship-as-history, leading to attachment, tact and discretion.
Depends / verdict: the floor's confidentiality. Speaker recognition can come later. Mutate, and dissolve the second trust.

8. ACTING, AND FINDING OUT WHAT HAPPENED (agency, consequence, failure, initiative)

Needs: success is the observed result. "Couldn't try", "tried and failed", "tried, can't verify" and "worked" are different experiences. Several routes can reach an outcome, and which she used is itself experience.
Try:
Outcome-first tasks on your phone (music, YouTube, alarm, notifications, a drafted message).
The same goal with weak resources, with the network cut, with a route removed, with the main route broken.
Repeated success becoming a reflex with measured savings.
Risky acts ask first.
Claims but doesn't: the action layer exists but nothing she says can reach it. Capability routing uses closed categories. I found no app-launch or alarm code.
Proof / breaks: results verified in the world. It breaks if success means "intent sent".
Replaces → emerges: closed categories replaced by outcome-described routes, leading to reflexes, route preferences and recovery.
Depends / verdict: the floor's approvals. Mutate, and dissolve closed categories when beaten.

9. LOOKING AT HERSELF (self-observation, reflection, honest uncertainty)

10. REST AND REORGANIZATION (sleep, consolidation)

Teach her inconsistent things, and after rest she is more coherent on held-out cases.
A repeated slow task becomes cheap at equal quality.

WHEN THE SEED IS READY
These are conditions, not subsystems. Each holds in minimal form:

one individual continuous across replacement
real lived experience
a persistent change that alters later behavior without replaying the episode
open interpretation
a real-world consequence she observes
self-observation with lineage
swappable mechanisms
resource-adaptive computation
a path from capability gap to approved capability
the first sign of plastic machinery
the first relationship developing from history
the floor intact

None gets its own permanent subsystem.
THE TWO KINDS OF SELF-IMPROVEMENT
Capability expansion (area 12) and cognitive development (areas 10 and 11) stay alive from the seed. Builder helps with the first. The second is the deeper problem, and rest plus the plasticity experiments are where it's attacked.
THE OLD HUMAN CORE
It's a candidate, not the center. A part is replaced only when the new behavior beats it in the same lived scenarios:

The residue test against the mood and trust tables.
The two-person drill against the trust enum.
The ambiguity drills against the statement fallback.
Outcome-settled routes against closed categories.
Truthful recall, which the guard would flag today. It flags phrases like "last time you told me" without checking any record.

Until a part is beaten, it stays as one candidate among others.
HOW IT RUNS IN PARALLEL
Every area is an open investigation from day one. A worker owns a question, not a folder, an organ or a name.

All workers use the same living Friday and the same history. One weekly integration judges the evidence.
Nobody waits except on a dependency named in the map.
If two investigations touch the same production file, they define the smallest seam.
If the free model pool wobbles, run fewer workers, never fewer questions.
Voice leads, because daily life is what starts her history.

HOW WE KNOW SHE'S DEVELOPING
These are signs, not criteria to hard-code:

She behaves differently because of what she lived, the difference survives a restart, and the old episode needn't be replayed.
She can say where a change came from.
Her preferences cohere with her history, and her reading of a person gets richer.
She has recurring ways of responding and can change them when reality contradicts them.
She notices things unprompted, can disagree, and surprises us.
She builds something nobody programmed.
She gets a capability because she saw a need.
She finds a better way of thinking, not just another tool.
She stays the same through model, device and resource changes.

THE TEST THAT MATTERS
Give her an outcome nobody built her for, with no method. Does she understand what's meant and notice the uncertainty? Does she work out what would satisfy it, find resources, and see what she lacks? Does she research, build or acquire a way forward, and test it? Does she ask permission only where the floor requires? Does she act, observe, learn, change, and remain the same Friday?
WHEN (weeks from today, and they slip if the free model pool stays flaky)

First living seed, 2–4 weeks: a persistent Friday in your daily use, the voice loop with today's parts, verified phone actions, her history accumulating with lineage, and the first A/B proof running.
First convincing human-like behavior: it starts the day she's lived with. The first signs are targeted for weeks 3–8: residue, a preference she can trace, an unprompted useful mention, and different handling of you and your sister.
First machine-superhuman abilities, weeks 6–16: as construction and resource reach mature, she gets abilities built, uses a workshop for heavy work, and has recall and search no person has.
Long-term depth: no date, because it's lived history.

The target is the closest approximation of your vision that today's technology allows. She absorbs better methods as they appear.
THE FLOOR
Authorization, identity protection, owner authentication, approval, security, rollback, continuity protection and provenance stay rigid. They aren't weakened for emergence, and they aren't the architecture either.
Each week the check is yours: does she feel more like someone, and did anything become permanent that isn't the floor?
