3. What makes her a developing person (PROPOSAL)
FACT, the starting point. Today experience can only change prompt text and the surface of a reply. Intent is always STATEMENT, nothing is predicted (TurnTraceRecord.predictions is hard-coded empty), and thresholds are flat. The Human Core wraps the reasoning instead of taking part in it.
Because the language model is a frozen organ, development has to live in what decides around it: what she notices and remembers, what she expects, how she reads you, what she tries, what she computes with, and what she has learned to do cheaply. One generic mechanism with six properties, not a psychology module:

Episodes. Everything she does leaves a record: the situation, what she considered, what she chose, what she expected, when.
Choices, not rules. Wherever behavior could go more than one way, the code opens a choice: how to read your words, which memories enter the prompt, how to answer, which model or tier computes, which tool, whether to act or ask. A learned preference makes it (a generic online learner, replaceable), with small bounded exploration. Today's hard-coded rules are just the first alternatives and the baseline.
Settlement. When something observable happens, it is settled against the choices and expectations that led to it, including delayed ones: your tap (landed / missed), a correction, a re-ask, thanks, task success or failure, time to answer, heat, degraded service. That yields surprise (prediction error) and credit or blame.
Valence. Surprise and outcomes leave a persistent, decaying appraisal. That is her mood: any choice may use it as context, and which choices it affects is learned, not fixed. This replaces word-counting "emotion". Her motives, what counts as good, are the only authored seeds. Defaults I chose, yours to change: be right about you and the world; your felt satisfaction; get things done; stay coherent with her commitments and the floor.
Consolidation. While idle or charging she replays episodes, generalizes across them, updates durable preferences and prunes, so the change survives losing the original memory. Every durable change goes through the continuity law with the episodes cited, accepted according to the evidence already behind the current value, not a counter or timer.
Anticipation (the inner voice). Between turns she predicts what you'll need (topic, time, action), pre-stages context and pre-wakes what's needed. Predictions are settled by what you actually do. The first predictions are cheap: frequency and time-of-day, no big model.

Why this is liquid. Any code can open a choice. Any mechanism can offer alternatives or learn the preference: a rule, a cache, a small classifier, a bandit, a synthesized program, a distilled model, an outside model. Nothing owns an outcome. The fixed parts are the floor and one law: no durable change without a recorded episode behind it.
Language. Outcomes come from taps and behavior, not English word lists, so Egyptian Arabic works through the same path. Every Life Test scenario has an English and an Egyptian-Arabic variant.
