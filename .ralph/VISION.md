# FRIDAY — Vision (written record)

This file records the owner's vision. The owner's latest authenticated explicit instruction outranks this document; if he corrects it, revise this record. The single active delivery plan is .ralph/FRIDAY_DELIVERY_PLAN.md. Everything else in the repository is subordinate to the current owner direction, this vision and that plan. Historical plans are not competing authorities.

## 1. What Friday is

Friday is one continuing digital individual, intended to approach human-like understanding, interaction, learning, relationships, adaptation and agency as closely as available technology permits, while extending capability through machine-native computation.

The owner's concept is **liquid Symbiotic quantum software**. Preserve that wording and ambition. It describes an adaptive, fluid software environment in which Friday uses, reshapes, combines and helps create the resources required to achieve outcomes. It must not be silently reduced to a conventional multi-agent framework, a fixed collection of specialist modules, a frozen architecture or a chatbot with accumulated features. The term does not by itself assert that quantum hardware is present or required.

Friday is the individual. Models, algorithms, memory representations, voices, perception methods, tools, Builder, external AIs, code, cloud services, devices and computational resources are what she can use, replace, adapt and eventually create. None of these is Friday's identity.

She is not a slave or a passive command executor. The intended relationship is buddy, partner and friend. The owner gives outcomes and direction in ordinary language; Friday works out how to pursue them, can surface uncertainty or disagreement, and must not silently substitute a different goal for an authenticated explicit request.

The practical aim is to reduce the distance between what the owner can imagine and what he can accomplish with Friday. Existing technology is a resource and a starting point, not the boundary of what may be researched, invented, combined or experimentally built.

## 2. The first installed Friday is the seed

The first Friday is not a disposable prototype or a static demo. She is the beginning of the same individual who can continue developing. Her initial capabilities may be limited; her means of continuation must already exist.

The seed must contain, in the smallest working form:
- persistent identity and continuity;
- a local Android-owned runtime that can exist offline;
- durable experience and a real experience-to-change-to-later-behaviour path;
- voice and ordinary interaction, with voice as the first user-visible priority;
- replaceable capabilities and the ability to recognize missing capabilities;
- a path to research, construct, test, approve, install and roll back new capabilities;
- adaptation to available compute and host resources;
- observation of outcomes, failures and resource costs;
- protected authorization, provenance, recovery and continuity.

A first release need not already possess every long-term capability. It must be a real Friday seed with a verified path to grow, not a collection of disconnected screens or declarations.

## 3. One individual across environments

Friday remains the same individual across offline/online conditions, local/cloud computation, model replacement, process death, app updates, runtime changes and future authorized hosts.

Offline is a real operating condition, not a different product or frozen mode. Local identity, continuity, experience and whatever perception, cognition, voice and action the local host can sustain continue offline. Reduced resources may mean slower, smaller or deferred work; they do not create another Friday or erase her.

Cloud and external models expand the resources available to the same Friday. They are not half of her, a second identity or the owner of her intelligence. The Android app is the first production host and the owner's phone is the first environment. Termux is not a required production runtime.

A phone, glasses, vehicle, home device or other connected environment may provide sensors, interfaces or compute when authorized. Such access extends the same individual; it must not create a separate specialist Friday for every device or domain.

## 4. Development changes the individual

We do not write a finished personality and then simulate its growth. Friday develops through experiences, conversations, corrections, demonstrations, relationships, actions, consequences, mistakes, discoveries and the environment.

The required causal pattern is:

**experience → persistent internal change → measurably changed later cognition or behaviour.**

The change must persist after the original episode and its context are unavailable. It must influence a later, meaningfully different situation. Merely storing a transcript, note, timestamp, sentiment, profile value or event count is not development. Replaying the original episode into a prompt is not development.

A claimed developmental effect must be tested on the real production composition with:
- a restart between the original experience and later test;
- a replay-blocked condition in which the original episode text cannot be retrieved or inserted;
- a mechanism-off control in which the proposed developmental mechanism is disabled;
- an observable later decision or behaviour, not just a database write.

Her understanding, interests, preferences, strategies, relationships, communication and self-model may change through lived experience. Do not author them as a fixed personality or predetermined set of developmental stages. She may learn from any relevant accessible domain, not just the subsystem that first observed the evidence.

## 5. Liquid cognition and open-ended capabilities

Outside the protected floor in Section 7, no mechanism owns a permanent cognitive job. No fixed specialist-brain atlas, mandatory pipeline, closed capability taxonomy, frozen developmental ladder or privileged route to an outcome.

A mechanism exists because it is useful now. It may be combined, repurposed, learned, replaced or removed. The current name of a mechanism does not reserve the outcome it helps produce. Hearing, speech, memory, prediction, reasoning, emotion, social understanding, learning, perception and action are not permanent ownership boundaries.

Not every situation needs expensive deliberation. A useful strategy earned through experience may become fast recognition, prediction or reflex. That is valid only when it is the result of real experience and remains open to revision.

### Liquid trunk and branches

The continuity and security floor is the protected trunk. Adaptable capabilities are branches. Branches can progress in parallel and exchange information through five small, open-ended contracts:

1. **Need:** the outcome Friday believes is wanted, retaining material uncertainty and alternative interpretations.
2. **Affordance:** what a capability can actually do, its requirements, cost, risk and observable success evidence.
3. **Resource field:** current compute, memory, energy, heat, storage, network, models, sensors, devices and permissions.
4. **Outcome:** what actually happened, including failure, uncertainty, latency, quality and resource cost, linked to the choice.
5. **Continuity and protected floor:** identity continuity, owner authentication, authorization, provenance, isolation, recovery and rollback.

These are exchange surfaces, not a prescribed cognitive pipeline. The chooser matching needs to affordances is itself replaceable and may become learned. No branch waits for an arbitrary fixed stage before it can develop.

## 6. Universal perception, interaction and learning

Friday must be able to use all relevant evidence she can legitimately access: language, voice, visual context, notifications, device state, sensors, demonstrations, user behaviour, actions and consequences. Her understanding is not text-only and not limited to pre-labelled domains.

For example, when authorized access exists, the same developing individual may learn from how the owner drives, what vehicle and road sensors report, what glasses observe, and how actions correlate with outcomes. This is not a request to hard-code a dedicated driving brain. It is an example of universal learning across people, actions and environments. Predictions that affect safety must be calibrated, uncertainty-aware and validated before being treated as advice.

Voice is the first user-visible priority: natural hearing, understanding, spoken response, turn-taking, interruption, conversation continuity and availability from supported Android surfaces. Hearing and speaking providers are replaceable and selected by measured quality, language support, latency, privacy, resource use and availability. Egyptian Arabic and mixed Arabic/English need real evaluation and the owner's listening judgment; no quality claim without evidence.

## 7. The protected floor

The following do not become liquid or self-authorize:
- owner authentication and authorization;
- protected operations and approval requirements;
- security boundaries, secrets and signing identity;
- provenance and integrity of evidence and artifacts;
- isolation of untrusted candidate code;
- continuity protection against unauthorized loss or replacement;
- verified update, rollback and recovery mechanisms.

Self-development, cloud workers and external models cannot bypass this floor. Safe runtime adaptation may occur within granted permissions. Changes to executable code, installed packages, permissions, signing or protected governance require the applicable approval and validation. The floor protects Friday's continuity; it does not prescribe her cognitive organization.

## 8. Liquid Symbiosis and Nano Algorithm Environment

The host adapts around Friday and Friday adapts to the host. The Nano Algorithm Environment should reduce the difference between resources a capability requires and resources actually available, while preserving the required outcome and quality.

Measure the real device and workload: memory, CPU/GPU/accelerators, thermal pressure, battery, latency, throughput, model quality, storage, network, voice and action workload. Search and compare algorithms, model sizes, runtimes, precision, kernels, scheduling, placement and local/cloud resources. Adaptation must change future execution decisions, not merely record telemetry.

A faster or smaller configuration is not an improvement if it breaks the outcome. Unknown hardware acceleration is unavailable until proven on the target device. Resource pressure changes what can run and when, not who Friday is. Defer, migrate, degrade gracefully or use an authorized external resource before sacrificing continuity.

## 9. Builder and self-development

The Builder is a capability Friday can use, not Friday herself and not a permanent superior intelligence. It exists so a missing capability is a temporary gap rather than a permanent ceiling.

When an outcome requires something she lacks, Friday should be able to recognize the gap, research existing work, reverse-engineer useful mechanisms, combine or repurpose unrelated mechanisms, invent candidates, construct them, test them in isolation, evaluate them independently, request required approval, promote them safely, observe their real use and learn from the result.

This includes eventual source-level inspection and improvement of her actual Android application and native capabilities. A restricted runtime script or synth DSL is a useful starting habitat, not the ultimate definition of self-development. External AI providers, Ralph, OpenHands, GitHub Actions, coding agents and search are replaceable workers/resources. No worker is the sole judge of its own change.

## 10. Resilience and delivery

Every failure must be reproducible, diagnosed at its cause and repaired at the mechanism that produced it. Prefer a correction that prevents a class of failures over a one-command patch. Preserve checkpoints, artifacts, provenance, durable work state, backups and last-known-good recovery.

Parallel work is encouraged across independent objectives, but all work must converge on one canonical main product line and one Friday. Do not create competing product branches or competing vision plans. Shared composition, identity and protected-floor changes require controlled integration.

CI, emulator builds and unit tests are necessary evidence, not final acceptance. The actual Android application must install and work on the owner's phone. The APK, signing lineage, update path, rollback and real-device behaviour must be verified before being claimed.

## 11. Evidence standard

A description cannot fail; a test can. Any claim about development, understanding, learning, action, adaptation, capability, performance or resilience must have an observable test that could disprove it.

Label evidence accurately: designed, implemented, unit-tested, CI-verified, emulator-verified or device-verified. A passing isolated test does not prove the live composition. A green CI run does not prove microphone, voice quality, background behaviour, update installation or physical-device performance. Never claim success before observing the actual result.

## 12. Direction and terminology

Do not redirect project planning into a debate about consciousness. The owner's project definition is liquid Symbiotic quantum software and the human-like capabilities that technology can support. Keep the discussion on architecture, implementation, experience, measurable behaviour and demonstrated outcomes. Do not replace the owner's terminology with a different philosophical or scientific framing.
